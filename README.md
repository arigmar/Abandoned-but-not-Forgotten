## About the Project

Since the 1960s, NASA has left hardware across the solar system: on the Moon, on Mars and in deep space. School-age space enthusiasts often hear only about active missions, while the machines that came before them remain silent.

**Abandoned but not Forgotten** is an interactive web page designed as a digital museum for kids. It brings together:

- A short **welcome animation**
- **Science cards** about each piece of hardware
- A **mini-game** built in TurboWarp

Built for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge**: *Abandoned but not Forgotten*.

## The Game

The player pilots a small **historian drone** across the Moon, Mars and deep space to reactivate the black boxes of real missions, such as:

- The **ALSEP** packages from the Apollo missions
- **Opportunity**
- **Ingenuity**
- **Pioneer 10**

To unlock each story, the player solves a micro-challenge based on the real engineering of the machine, such as cleaning the dust off its solar panels or calibrating a seismometer. Once the challenge is solved, the robot comes to life and tells, in the first person, the scientific discovery it left to humanity.

## Rewards

- **Badges:** one for every robot the player reactivates.
- **Museum showcase:** silhouettes that fill in with color as each robot is rescued.
- **Collectible fact cards:** every rescue unlocks a card with a real fact about the mission.
- **Final certificate:** awarded after collecting every badge.

- [Astro 5](https://astro.build/)

- [Preact](https://preactjs.com/)
- [Globe.gl](https://globe.gl/)
- [GSAP](https://gsap.com/)

**Game**
- **Engine:** [TurboWarp](https://turbowarp.org/), a Scratch 3.0 mod
- **Language / Output:** block-based visual programming, compiled to HTML5 / JavaScript with the [TurboWarp Packager](https://packager.turbowarp.org/)
